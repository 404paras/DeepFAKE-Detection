\chapter{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{chap:conclusion-future}

\section{Conclusion}

This thesis presented a unified study of deepfake detection across two connected problem settings. The first contribution developed a hybrid visual detector that combines ResNet50, Bidirectional GRUs, and Multi-Head Attention to improve spatial-temporal deepfake classification on an aggregated visual benchmark. The second contribution expanded the detection problem to multimodal media and introduced a synchronicity-aware architecture that models both modality-specific dynamics and cross-modal inconsistency through attention-based fusion.

Taken together, the thesis argues for a broader view of deepfake forensics. Effective detection is not only a matter of choosing a strong classifier. It also depends on benchmark diversity, temporal reasoning, modality selection, and defensible interpretation of reported performance. The combined results show that visual-temporal hybrid modeling remains valuable, while multimodal analysis becomes increasingly necessary as synthetic media grows more realistic and coordinated.

\section{Future Work}

Several directions follow naturally from the present work:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
    \item Extend both studies with stronger cross-dataset testing, confidence intervals, and statistical uncertainty estimates so that generalization claims can be measured more rigorously rather than inferred only from point estimates.
    \item Improve the multimodal pipeline for highly compressed, low-resolution, and noisy audio-visual inputs, where real-world deployment conditions remain challenging and where frequency-aware or resolution-robust methods may be especially useful \cite{Kim2024Freq}.
    \item Expand fairness-aware evaluation through explicit subgroup reporting, calibration analysis, and fairness-aware optimization rather than summary-level statements alone \cite{Ju2024Fairness,fu2025unbiased}.
    \item Explore transformer-based and other long-range sequence models for both visual and multimodal reasoning, especially where contextual cross-modal attention may outperform recurrent alternatives \cite{Katamneni2024}.
    \item Add systematic error analysis and explainability tooling so that future detectors can report not only whether media is suspicious, but also which temporal or cross-modal cues drove the decision.
\end{enumerate}

These extensions would strengthen the transition from paper-scale experimental studies to a more deployment-ready deepfake forensic framework.
