% Instructions for Adding Logo to Title Page and Certificate
% ==========================================================

To add the NIT Kurukshetra logo to your thesis:

1. OBTAIN THE LOGO:
   - Get the official NIT Kurukshetra logo (usually named: nitkkr_logo.png or similar)
   - Place it in the thesis directory or in a subdirectory like: thesis/images/logo.png

2. UNCOMMENT THE LOGO LINES IN main.tex:

   For TITLE PAGE (center, after supervisor name):
   Find this line in main.tex around line 185:

   % \includegraphics[width=3cm]{logo.png}\\[1cm]

   Change it to:

   \includegraphics[width=3cm]{images/logo.png}\\[1cm]

   (Adjust the path if your logo is in a different location)

3. For CERTIFICATE PAGE (upper left corner):
   Find these lines in main.tex around line 200:

   % \begin{tikzpicture}[remember picture,overlay]
   %     \node[anchor=north west,xshift=1in,yshift=-1in] at (current page.north west)
   %     {\includegraphics[width=2cm]{logo.png}};
   % \end{tikzpicture}

   Remove the % to uncomment:

   \begin{tikzpicture}[remember picture,overlay]
       \node[anchor=north west,xshift=1in,yshift=-1in] at (current page.north west)
       {\includegraphics[width=2cm]{images/logo.png}};
   \end{tikzpicture}

4. COMPILE:
   cd thesis
   pdflatex main.tex
   pdflatex main.tex

CURRENT FRONT MATTER STRUCTURE:
===============================

Page 1: Title Page (with border)
  - M.Tech Dissertation Part-I MCO2D01
  - Title of the work
  - Your name and roll number
  - Supervisor name
  - [Logo in center] (uncomment when you have the logo)
  - Department, Institute details
  - Date

Page 2: Certificate Page (with border)
  - [Logo in upper left corner] (uncomment when you have the logo)
  - CERTIFICATE heading
  - Student's certification statement
  - Student signature
  - Supervisor's certification statement
  - Supervisor signature

Page 3: Declaration (if needed from frontmatter/declaration.tex)

Then continues with:
  - Abstract
  - Acknowledgements
  - List of Abbreviations
  - Table of Contents
  - List of Figures
  - List of Tables
  - Chapters

ADJUSTING LOGO SIZE:
====================
- For center logo: Change width=3cm to your preferred size (e.g., width=4cm)
- For corner logo: Change width=2cm to your preferred size (e.g., width=2.5cm)

ADJUSTING BORDER:
=================
The border is set at 0.75 inches from page edge. To change:
- Find: xshift=0.75in,yshift=-0.75in
- Change to: xshift=1in,yshift=-1in (for thicker border spacing)

BORDER LINE THICKNESS:
=====================
Current: line width=2pt
Change to: line width=1pt (thinner) or line width=3pt (thicker)
